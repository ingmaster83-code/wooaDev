💡 Tips

  • Large text: 18pt (24px) or larger, or 14pt (~18.67px) Bold or heavier.
  • AA: The minimum standard required by most legal accessibility requirements.
  • AAA: The highest level of accessibility. May not be achievable for all content.
  • Low contrast makes text hard to read for users with low vision or color blindness.
What is WCAG?

Web Content Accessibility Guidelines, published by the W3C. It defines two levels of conformance: AA and AAA. Most organizations and regulations require at least AA compliance.

How is contrast ratio calculated?

Based on relative luminance: (L1 + 0.05) / (L2 + 0.05) where L1 is the brighter color. The maximum ratio is 21:1 (black vs white) and minimum is 1:1 (identical colors).

What are UI component requirements?

Non-text elements like button borders, input outlines, and icons also need at least 3:1 contrast against their background (WCAG 2.1 AA success criterion 1.4.11).
