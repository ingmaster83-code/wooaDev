💡 Tips

  • Large text: 18pt (24px) or larger, or 14pt (~18.67px) Bold or heavier.
  • AA: The minimum standard required by most legal accessibility requirements.
  • AAA: The highest level of accessibility. May not be achievable for all content.
  • Low contrast makes text hard to read for users with low vision or color blindness.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between WCAG AA and AAA?

AA requires a 4.5:1 contrast ratio for normal text (3:1 for large text). AAA requires 7:1 (4.5:1 for large text).

How do I improve a low contrast ratio?

Make the background lighter or the text color darker — or vice versa — until the ratio meets the required threshold.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. All processing happens locally in your browser. Nothing is transmitted to any server.
