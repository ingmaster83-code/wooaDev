DNS Record Lookup (DoH)

Enter a domain or IP to query Cloudflare or Google DoH resolvers for A, AAAA, CNAME, MX, TXT, NS, SOA, CAA and more. See TTL, RCODE, DNSSEC (AD) flags and the raw JSON, and compare the two resolvers' answers if you like.

Example

✅ 1 lookup(s) · 2 record(s)

Input — Domain or IP (one per line, up to 5)

example.com

Result — Answer records

Name | Type | TTL (s) | Data | Resolver
example.com. | A | 300 | 93.184.216.34 | cloudflare
example.com. | A | 300 | 93.184.216.35 | cloudflare

Result — Response status

Query | Type | Resolver | RCODE | Flags | Answers | Authority | Note
example.com | A | cloudflare | NOERROR | RA RD | 2 | 0 | OK

What this tool does

Check DNS from the browser without dig. Queries go straight to the public DoH resolver you choose, not to WooaHouse.

  • Types: A, AAAA, CNAME, MX, TXT, NS, SOA, CAA, SRV, PTR, DS, DNSKEY, HTTPS, SVCB, TLSA, NAPTR, or all common types at once
  • Resolvers: Cloudflare (1.1.1.1), Google (8.8.8.8), or compare both (differences are flagged)
  • Response: name, type, TTL and data table, RCODE (NXDOMAIN, SERVFAIL ...), AD / RD / RA flags and raw JSON
  • DNSSEC: request the DO bit, disable validation (CD) and check the AD flag
  • An IP address is turned into an in-addr.arpa / ip6.arpa PTR reverse lookup automatically

💡 Tips

  • On SERVFAIL, retry with 'Disable DNSSEC validation' to tell a signing problem from a resolver problem.
  • TXT records split into several strings are joined into one, which is handy for checking SPF and DKIM.
  • Right after a DNS change the two resolvers may differ because of caching; wait for the TTL and check again.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does it differ from dig?

dig uses your local resolver while this tool uses public DoH resolvers. Caching and regional answers can differ.

What if the lookup fails?

A corporate network or browser extension may block the DoH domains. Choose the other resolver or check your network policy.

Is the domain I look up sent anywhere?

Your browser queries the public DoH resolver you choose (Cloudflare or Google) directly, not a WooaHouse server. The name you look up is therefore visible to that resolver, so avoid private internal domains. WooaHouse does not store anything you enter.
