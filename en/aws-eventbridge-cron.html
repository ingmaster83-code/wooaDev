AWS EventBridge Schedule Expressions — cron() & rate()

What this tool does

AWS scheduled rules use a six-field format (minute hour day month weekday year) wrapped in cron( ), which differs from ordinary cron and is easy to get wrong for Lambda or ECS schedules.

  • One of day-of-month and day-of-week must be ?, as in cron(0 18 ? * MON-FRI *)
  • rate(1 minute) vs rate(5 minutes): the unit must be singular for 1 and plural otherwise
  • Supports L, W, # and the year field (1970–2199)
  • Rules are always evaluated in UTC; the tool also converts run times to your time zone
  • Presets and clear error messages help you fix a rule quickly

💡 Tips

  • EventBridge Rules only support UTC. For 9 AM in Seoul write cron(0 0 * * ? *).
  • EventBridge Scheduler accepts a time zone and handles daylight saving; prefer it when you can.
  • rate counts from rule creation, so use cron when a run has to land on the hour.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do I always need a ?

Day-of-month and day-of-week cannot both be specified, so one must be ?. Daily at noon is cron(0 12 * * ? *).

What is the shortest interval?

One minute for both rate and cron. For anything faster, loop inside a single invocation or look at Step Functions.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. Everything is processed inside your browser; what you enter is never sent to or stored on a server, so you can safely paste data containing personal information, internal settings or API keys.
