💡 Tips

  • Use special strings like @daily or @hourly instead of manual cron syntax for common schedules.
  • The 5 fields are: minute, hour, day of month, month, day of week — in that order.
  • Use a comma to list multiple values and a hyphen to specify a range (e.g., 1-5 for Monday–Friday).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is cron?

Cron is a Unix/Linux job scheduler that runs commands or scripts automatically at specified times.

How do I run a job every 15 minutes?

Use */15 in the minute field: */15 * * * * /path/to/command.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. All processing happens entirely within your browser — your files are never uploaded to any server. This zero-server architecture keeps your data 100% private, safe for sensitive documents and enterprise security environments.
