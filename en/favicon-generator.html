Frequently Asked Questions

Should I use favicon.ico or PNG files?

Use both. favicon.ico ensures compatibility with older browsers, while PNG files are better for high-DPI displays and PWAs. Download the ZIP and paste the included HTML snippet — each browser will automatically pick the best format.

What image size should I start with?

A square image of at least 512×512px is recommended. PNG or SVG formats give the best results. After uploading, all sizes are generated automatically.

Is my file uploaded to a server?

No. All processing happens entirely within your browser — your files are never uploaded to any server. This zero-server architecture keeps your data 100% private, safe for sensitive documents and enterprise security environments.
