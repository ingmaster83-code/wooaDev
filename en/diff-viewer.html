💡 Tips

  • Paste two versions of a file to see exactly what changed between them.
  • Green lines are additions; red lines are deletions.
  • Great for code reviews, comparing config files, or spotting typos in documents.

Frequently Asked Questions

What file types can I compare?

Any text-based content: source code, JSON, YAML, Markdown, plain text, and more.

Is the diff shown line-by-line or character-by-character?

Line-by-line by default. Changed lines are highlighted so you can quickly identify differences.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. All processing happens locally in your browser. Nothing is transmitted to any server.
