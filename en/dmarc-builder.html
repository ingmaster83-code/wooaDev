DMARC Record Builder & Analyzer

Pick p, sp, pct, rua, adkim and aspf to build the _dmarc TXT record, or analyse an existing record for errors and weaknesses. It also shows which policy applies to subdomains.

Example

✅ DMARC record built (_dmarc.example.com)

Input — DMARC record to check

v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:dmarc@example.com; pct=50; adkim=r

Result — DMARC TXT value

v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:dmarc@example.com

_dmarc.example.com.  IN  TXT  "v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:dmarc@example.com"

Result — Tag breakdown

Tag | Value | Meaning
v | DMARC1 | Version
p | none | Domain policy
rua | mailto:dmarc@example.com | Aggregate report destinations
Effective — example.com | none | Applies to mail from this domain
Effective — *.example.com | none | No sp, so p is inherited

What this tool does

DMARC checks SPF and DKIM results for alignment with the From domain, and declares what to do with failures (allow, quarantine, reject) and where to send reports.

  • Build: policy (p), subdomain policy (sp), percentage (pct), aggregate reports (rua), forensic reports (ruf), alignment (adkim / aspf), fo and ri
  • Analyse: v=DMARC1 position, missing p, invalid values, duplicate or unknown tags, mailto: format and pct range
  • Explains the authorisation record (domain._report._dmarc.receiver) needed to send reports to another domain
  • Shows the policy effectively applied to the domain and to *.domain (sp falls back to p)
  • Suggests the usual rollout from p=none through quarantine to reject

💡 Tips

  • Begin with p=none; rua=… for two to four weeks and confirm every legitimate sender passes SPF / DKIM alignment.
  • Raise pct in steps 10 → 25 → 50 → 100 to limit the risk of blocking genuine mail.
  • Report addresses on another domain need an authorisation TXT record on that domain.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is p=none useless?

It does not change mail handling but it delivers reports, which are essential to see your senders. To be protected you must move to quarantine or reject.

Does this relate to Gmail / Yahoo bulk sender rules?

Yes. Bulk senders need SPF and DKIM plus at least a p=none DMARC record. For real protection quarantine or stricter is recommended.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. Everything is processed inside your browser; what you enter is never sent to or stored on a server, so you can safely paste data containing personal information, internal settings or API keys.
