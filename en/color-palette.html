FAQ

What is a complementary color?
A complementary color is directly opposite on the color wheel (180° away in hue). It creates the highest visual contrast and is commonly used to highlight important elements like CTA buttons against a background.
Which color scheme is best for UI design?
Analogous and monochromatic schemes tend to look most professional and are easiest to work with. Use complementary or triadic colors for accent elements when you want something to stand out.
