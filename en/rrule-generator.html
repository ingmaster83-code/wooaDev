RRULE Generator & Explainer — iCalendar Recurrence Rules

Explains or builds the RRULE recurrence of calendar events (Google, Outlook, iCal) and previews the dates it really produces. Supports BYDAY, BYSETPOS, COUNT and UNTIL.

Example

✅ Valid RRULE

Input — RRULE (iCalendar recurrence rule)

RRULE:FREQ=MONTHLY;BYDAY=MO,TU,WE,TH,FR;BYSETPOS=-1;COUNT=6

Result — RRULE

RRULE:FREQ=MONTHLY;BYDAY=MO,TU,WE,TH,FR;BYSETPOS=-1;COUNT=6

Result — Interpretation

Summary: every month · Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri · taking position -1 of the matching set · 10:00 · 6 times in total
DTSTART: 2026-10-16T10:00:00 (floating local time)
Occurrences shown: 6

What this tool does

Recurring events in calendar apps and .ics files are expressed by a single RRULE line. Read one, or build your own and check that the dates come out as intended.

  • Parse mode: paste RRULE:FREQ=WEEKLY;BYDAY=MO,WE,FR;COUNT=5 to get a sentence and the list of occurrences
  • Build mode: pick frequency, interval, weekdays, month days, months, BYSETPOS and the end (count / until) in a form
  • Advanced rules such as 1MO (first Monday), -1FR (last Friday) and BYSETPOS=-1 (last weekday) are supported
  • Calculates N occurrences from DTSTART and shows the weekday
  • Invalid combinations are explained

💡 Tips

  • The last weekday of every month is FREQ=MONTHLY;BYDAY=MO,TU,WE,TH,FR;BYSETPOS=-1.
  • Give UNTIL in the same form as DTSTART (date or UTC time) so strict calendars accept it.
  • COUNT and UNTIL cannot be combined; use one of them.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is RRULE different from cron?

cron is a time pattern; RRULE expresses calendar rules such as the second Tuesday of each month or every other week. For minute or second level schedules cron fits better.

What about EXDATE and RDATE?

This tool covers the RRULE itself. Excluded (EXDATE) and extra (RDATE) dates go on separate lines of the .ics file.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. Everything is processed inside your browser; what you enter is never sent to or stored on a server, so you can safely paste data containing personal information, internal settings or API keys.
