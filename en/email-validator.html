Frequently Asked Questions

Does this confirm the email address actually exists?

No, it only validates syntax/format. To confirm deliverability, you'd need to send a verification email.

What typos does it detect?

It compares your domain against common providers like gmail.com and outlook.com and suggests a correction when the spelling is close, e.g. gmial.com → gmail.com.

Are inputs stored on a server?

No. All processing happens entirely in your browser and inputs are never sent to a server.
