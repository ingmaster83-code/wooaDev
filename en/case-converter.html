💡 Tips

  • camelCase is common in JavaScript variables; PascalCase is standard for class names.
  • snake_case is the convention in Python, Ruby, and database column names.
  • SCREAMING_SNAKE_CASE is typically used for constants in most programming languages.

Frequently Asked Questions

When do I use camelCase vs snake_case?

camelCase is prevalent in JavaScript and Java; snake_case is standard in Python, Ruby, and SQL.

Does the converter handle unicode characters?

Yes. The converter handles unicode letters and preserves non-alphabetic characters as separators.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. All processing happens locally in your browser. Nothing is transmitted to any server.
