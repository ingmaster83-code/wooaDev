What this tool does

"How long will a 4.7 GB file take on a 500 Mbps connection?" — answered instantly, with the bit/byte confusion taken care of.

  • Sizes: 4.7 GB, 700MB, 1.5 TiB … (GB = 10^9 bytes, GiB = 2^30 bytes are distinguished)
  • Speeds: 100 Mbps, 1 Gbps (bits) or 12 MB/s (bytes) — capital B means bytes, lowercase b bits
  • Efficiency: real transfers are slower than the theoretical rate because of protocol overhead and congestion (default 90%)
  • A comparison table from 10 Mbps to 10 Gbps, USB 3.0, HDD and NVMe SSD

💡 Tips

  • ISP speeds are advertised in bits (Mbps). Divide by 8 for megabytes per second: 100 Mbps ≈ 12.5 MB/s.
  • Wi-Fi often delivers half the nominal rate or less — try an efficiency of 50–70%.
  • SSD copy speeds approach their maximum only for large files.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between Mbps and MB/s?

Mbps is megabits per second, MB/s is megabytes per second. One byte is 8 bits, so 100 Mbps is 12.5 MB/s.

GB or GiB?

Windows Explorer often shows GiB as GB, while drive makers use GB (10^9). If you know which one applies, enter the exact unit.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. Everything is processed inside your browser; what you enter is never sent to or stored on a server, so you can safely paste data containing personal information, internal settings or API keys.
