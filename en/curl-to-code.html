Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I get a cURL command?

In your browser's dev tools Network tab, right-click a request → Copy → Copy as cURL.

Does it support every cURL option?

It supports method (-X), headers (-H), body (-d/--data), and basic auth (-u). Very specialized flags (like --cookie-jar) may not be recognized.

Is the cURL command I enter stored on a server?

No. All conversion happens entirely in your browser and inputs are never sent to a server.
