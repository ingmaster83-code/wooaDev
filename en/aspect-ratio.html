💡 Tips

  • 16:9: Standard for YouTube, most monitors and TVs
  • 4:3: Legacy TV and camera photo ratio
  • 1:1: Instagram square posts
  • Use aspect-ratio CSS property for responsive elements that maintain their ratio

FAQ

What is the relationship between 16:9 and 1920×1080?
1920 ÷ 1080 = 1.7778, and dividing by the GCD gives 16:9. So 1920×1080 (Full HD) is a 16:9 resolution. Other examples: 1280×720 (HD), 3840×2160 (4K UHD).
How do I use CSS aspect-ratio?
Write aspect-ratio: 16 / 9; on an element. If you set only the width, the height adjusts automatically. Great for responsive images, video wrappers, and card layouts.
