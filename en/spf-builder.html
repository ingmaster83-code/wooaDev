SPF Record Builder & Checker

List the servers that send mail for your domain to build the SPF TXT record, or check an existing one for syntax errors, risky settings such as ptr and +all, and the 10 DNS lookup limit.

Example

✅ SPF record built (3/10 DNS lookups)

Input — Sending sources (one per line)

mx
include:_spf.google.com
ip4:203.0.113.10
ip4:198.51.100.0/24
ip6:2001:db8::/32
a:mail.example.com

Input — SPF record to check

v=spf1 mx include:_spf.google.com include:spf.protection.outlook.com ptr ip4:203.0.113.10 ?all

Result — SPF TXT value

v=spf1 mx include:_spf.google.com ip4:203.0.113.10 ip4:198.51.100.0/24 ip6:2001:db8::/32 a:mail.example.com -all

example.com.  IN  TXT  "v=spf1 mx include:_spf.google.com ip4:203.0.113.10 ip4:198.51.100.0/24 ip6:2001:db8::/32 a:mail.example.com -all"

Result — Term breakdown

Term | Qualifier | Meaning | Lookups
mx | Pass | The MX hosts of this domain may send | 1
include:_spf.google.com | Pass | Includes the SPF policy of _spf.google.com (Google Workspace / Gmail) | 1
ip4:203.0.113.10 | Pass | Sending allowed from 203.0.113.10 (1 addresses) | 0
ip4:198.51.100.0/24 | Pass | Sending allowed from 198.51.100.0/24 (256 addresses) | 0
ip6:2001:db8::/32 | Pass | Sending allowed from 2001:db8::/32 (2^96 addresses) | 0
a:mail.example.com | Pass | The A/AAAA addresses of mail.example.com may send | 1
-all | Fail | Rejects mail from every server not listed above | 0

What this tool does

SPF declares in DNS which servers may send mail for your domain. A wrong record sends genuine mail to spam or lets anyone spoof you.

  • Build: enter mx, a, include:, ip4:, ip6: one per line and pick -all / ~all / ?all. Shortcuts: google, microsoft365, sendgrid, mailgun, ses, mailchimp, zoho
  • Check: v=spf1 start, unknown terms, terms after all that are ignored, duplicates and redirect-versus-all conflicts
  • Risky settings: +all (anyone may send), ?all (no protection), ptr (discouraged), 0.0.0.0/0 and CIDRs with host bits set
  • Counts DNS lookups (include, a, mx, ptr, exists, redirect) and warns when the limit of 10 is exceeded (PermError)
  • Over 255 characters the DNS value is printed as several quoted strings

💡 Tips

  • A domain may have only one SPF record. Add an include to the existing record instead of creating a second TXT.
  • Start with ~all (softfail), review DMARC reports for legitimate senders, then tighten to -all.
  • Lookups inside include also count. Near the limit, remove services you no longer use.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens beyond 10 lookups?

Receivers treat SPF as PermError and authentication fails. Nested includes (Google and others) add their own lookups, so the real limit is reached sooner.

Does this tool query DNS?

No, it analyses the text statically. Lookups nested in includes and the record actually published are not checked, so combine it with a DNS lookup tool.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. Everything is processed inside your browser; what you enter is never sent to or stored on a server, so you can safely paste data containing personal information, internal settings or API keys.
