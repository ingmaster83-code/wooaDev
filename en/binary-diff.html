Binary Compare (Hex Diff)

Paste two binaries as hex or Base64, or choose two files, to see the differing bytes grouped into ranges with offset, length and both values, plus a 16-byte-per-line hex diff where differing bytes are marked with *. Length differences and CRC-32 values are reported too.

Example

5 differing byte(s) in 3 range(s)

Input — A (hex / Base64)

7f 45 4c 46 02 01 01 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 03 00 3e 00 01 00 00 00 40 10 00 00 00 00 00 00 48 65 6c 6c 6f 2c 20 57 6f 6f 61 21 00 00 00 00

Input — B (hex / Base64)

7f 45 4c 46 02 01 01 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 03 00 b7 00 01 00 00 00 40 10 00 00 00 00 00 00 48 65 6c 6c 6f 2c 20 57 6f 72 6c 64 21 00 00 00 00 de ad

Result — Summary

Size of A: 48 B (CRC-32 46ff32ca)
Size of B: 51 B (CRC-32 1d8546ab)
Differing bytes: 5 / 48 (10.42%)
Difference ranges: 3
First difference: 0x12
Length difference: B is longer by 3 B

Result — Difference ranges

Offset range | Length | A | B
0x12 – 0x12 | 1 | 3e | b7
0x29 – 0x2c | 4 | 6f 61 21 00 | 72 6c 64 21
0x30 – 0x32 | 3 | (none) | 00 de ad

What this tool does

Find where a build, firmware image or config blob changed, in the browser. Your data is not uploaded.

  • Input: hex (spaces, 0x and colons allowed), Base64 or two files
  • Ranges: offset range, length, values from A and B, and an adjustable 'merge nearby differences' gap
  • Hex diff: only the lines around changes in 16-byte rows with differing bytes marked *, identical stretches collapsed
  • Summary: differing bytes and percentage, first difference, length difference and both CRC-32 values
  • Hints when only one spot differs or more than half the bytes differ

💡 Tips

  • When comparing reproducible builds that differ only in timestamps or build IDs, expect few short ranges.
  • Raise the context value to see more bytes around each change.
  • Use the Executable Header Viewer to learn what a given offset means in a header.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can it compare large files?

As far as browser memory allows. Tens of MB are usually fine, and the number of ranges shown is capped when there are very many differences.

How is it different from a text diff?

It compares by byte position, not by line. An inserted byte makes everything after it differ, because insertions and deletions are not aligned.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. Everything is processed inside your browser; what you enter is never sent to or stored on a server, so you can safely paste data containing personal information, internal settings or API keys.
