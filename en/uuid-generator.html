💡 Tips

  • UUID v4 is randomly generated and is the most commonly used version.
  • UUIDs are 128-bit values displayed as 32 hexadecimal digits in 5 groups separated by hyphens.
  • UUIDs are statistically unique without any central coordination — collision probability is negligible.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a UUID?

UUID (Universally Unique Identifier) is a 128-bit label used to uniquely identify resources in distributed systems.

What is the difference between UUID v1 and v4?

UUID v1 is based on time and MAC address; v4 is purely random. v4 is preferred for privacy and simplicity.
