Mobile Unit & Colour Converter (dp, sp, px, pt)

Converts dp, sp, px and pt for a screen density (mdpi to xxxhdpi) and computes per-density asset sizes. Colours are shown as #AARRGGBB, CSS rgba, Flutter Color, iOS UIColor, SwiftUI and Android XML at once.

What this tool does

No need to calculate units and colour notations by hand when moving design values across Android, iOS and Flutter.

  • Units: px = dp × (dpi ÷ 160), sp includes the font scale, pt is iOS's logical unit (treated as 1pt = 1dp)
  • Density table: pixel sizes for ldpi–xxxhdpi with drawable folders, and iOS @1x–@3x
  • Physical size (mm / inch) and minimum touch targets (Android 48 dp, iOS 44 pt)
  • Colour: #RRGGBB, #RRGGBBAA (CSS), #AARRGGBB (Android), rgba() and 0xAARRGGBB in → every notation plus HSL
  • Output: Flutter Color, UIColor, SwiftUI Color, Color.argb, Android XML and Compose

💡 Tips

  • 8-digit colours are #RRGGBBAA in CSS but #AARRGGBB in Android and Flutter; the alpha sits in opposite places.
  • Icons are often drawn at xxhdpi and scaled down for other densities.
  • sp follows the user's font setting; use it for text only and dp for layout sizes.

Frequently Asked Questions

dp versus sp?

dp is a density-independent length; sp is that multiplied by the user's font-size setting.

Is an iOS pt a pixel?

No. A point is a logical unit that becomes pixels at the device scale (@2x, @3x).

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. Everything is processed inside your browser; what you enter is never sent to or stored on a server, so you can safely paste data containing personal information, internal settings or API keys.
