NMEA 0183 GPS Sentence Parser

Decodes NMEA 0183 sentences such as $GPGGA and $GPRMC into latitude and longitude (decimal degrees), time, speed, heading, satellites, HDOP and altitude, and checks the XOR checksum. Map links are included.

What this tool does

Make GPS receiver serial output or logs readable. Everything is processed in your browser.

  • Sentences: GGA (fix data), RMC (recommended minimum), GLL, VTG and GSA (DOP and satellites in use); others list their fields
  • Coordinates: ddmm.mmmm → decimal degrees with N/S/E/W signs, map links
  • Values: UTC time and date, speed knots → km/h, course, satellites, HDOP, altitude and fix quality (RTK …)
  • Checksum: XOR of characters between $ and * compared with the hex after *
  • Talker IDs (GP, GN, GL, GA, BD) recognised; one sentence per line

💡 Tips

  • An RMC status of V (void) means the position is not valid; do not trust the coordinates.
  • Fix quality 0 means no fix; 4 (RTK fixed) and 5 (RTK float) are precision modes.
  • Sentences starting with GN combine several constellations (GPS + GLONASS …).

Frequently Asked Questions

What if the checksum fails?

It is probably a bit error in transit and the sentence is normally discarded. $ and * are excluded from the calculation.

Why is speed in knots?

NMEA comes from marine standards and uses knots (nautical miles per hour); 1 knot is 1.852 km/h.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. Everything is processed inside your browser; what you enter is never sent to or stored on a server, so you can safely paste data containing personal information, internal settings or API keys.
