What this tool does

Timeouts are in milliseconds, cache TTLs in seconds, config files say 1h 30m and APIs say PT1H30M. Enter a duration in any of these forms and see every unit.

  • ISO 8601: P1DT2H30M15.5S, PT90S, P2W (with years/months, 1 year = 365 days and 1 month = 30 days are used and flagged)
  • Unit text: 1h 30m 20s, 2 days 3 hours, 1.5 hours, 90s
  • Clock notation: 01:30:00.250, 03:25 (mm:ss); bare numbers use the unit you choose
  • Output: ms, seconds, minutes, hours, days, ISO 8601, human-readable text and hh:mm:ss

💡 Tips

  • JavaScript's setTimeout takes milliseconds and Redis TTLs are seconds — check unit slips here.
  • Enter several lines to compare them in a table.
  • Negative durations such as -PT30S are supported.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the M in an ISO 8601 duration mean months or minutes?

An M before the T is months (P1M); after the T it is minutes (PT1M). The T marks where the time part starts.

How many days is one month?

Months differ in length, so 30 days (and 365 for a year) are used as an approximation and flagged. Exact calendar math needs a reference date.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. Everything is processed inside your browser; what you enter is never sent to or stored on a server, so you can safely paste data containing personal information, internal settings or API keys.
