💡 Tips

  • Use \d for digits, \w for word characters, \s for whitespace.
  • Add the g flag for global matching, i for case-insensitive, m for multiline.
  • Test your pattern against multiple inputs to ensure it handles edge cases correctly.

Frequently Asked Questions

What regex engine does this use?

JavaScript RegExp, which is compatible with most modern browsers and Node.js.

How do I match a literal dot?

Escape it with a backslash: \.. Without escaping, . matches any character.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. All processing happens entirely within your browser — your files are never uploaded to any server. This zero-server architecture keeps your data 100% private, safe for sensitive documents and enterprise security environments.
