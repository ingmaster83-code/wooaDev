💡 About IP Address Information

  • IP geolocation shows an approximate location, not your exact home address.
  • Using a VPN or proxy will display the VPN server's location instead of yours.
  • Both IPv4 (e.g. 192.168.1.1) and IPv6 addresses are supported.
  • ISP (Internet Service Provider): the company that provides your internet connection.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does my IP location differ from my actual location?
IP geolocation databases use registration data from ISPs, which may point to the ISP's office rather than your physical address. VPN users will see the VPN server's location.
How does a company or school IP appear?
Institutional IPs typically show the organization's name in the ISP/Organization field, and the location reflects where the network is registered.
