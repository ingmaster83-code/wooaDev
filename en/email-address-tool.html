What this tool does

Two things you often need when cleaning a customer list or adding a contact link to a web page, in one tool.

  • Validate: local part rules (consecutive dots, 64 characters, quoted form), domain labels (63 characters, hyphen placement), all-numeric TLDs, IP literals and IDN domains (to punycode)
  • Separators: newlines, commas and semicolons; display names in "Name" <address> form are understood
  • Clean up: lowercase domains, remove duplicates, show the base address of +tag addresses and suggest fixes for typo domains
  • mailto: recipients, cc, bcc, subject and body encoded per RFC 6068 (spaces as %20, newlines as %0D%0A)
  • Non-English subjects and bodies are encoded safely and an HTML a tag is generated

💡 Tips

  • A well-formed address does not prove the mailbox exists. Send a confirmation email with a link to verify it.
  • mailto links over 2,000 characters get truncated by some mail apps; use a form for long bodies.
  • Publishing an address in plain HTML exposes it to spam harvesters; consider a contact form or obfuscation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it accept every RFC 5322 form?

It accepts quoted local parts and IP literals but leaves out comments ((…)) and obsolete syntax, which are almost never used in practice.

Is bcc in a mailto link safe?

The hidden address is written plainly in the link, so anyone viewing the page source sees it. Avoid it on public pages.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. Everything is processed inside your browser; what you enter is never sent to or stored on a server, so you can safely paste data containing personal information, internal settings or API keys.
