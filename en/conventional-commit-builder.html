Conventional Commits Builder & Checker

Enter the type, scope, description, body, breaking change and issue references to build a Conventional Commits message, or paste an existing message to check it against the rules.

✅ Follows Conventional Commits

auth

Replaces the implicit flow. Tokens are now exchanged on the server.

123

What this tool does

A consistent commit format makes changelogs and version bumps automatable. This tool means you do not have to memorise the format.

  • Build: type (feat, fix, docs, style, refactor, perf, test, build, ci, chore, revert), scope, description, body, breaking change and issue numbers (Fixes / Refs)
  • Check: first-line form, lowercase type, standard type, trailing period, capital start, past tense, space after the colon, subject length and blank line after the subject
  • SemVer impact: BREAKING → major, feat → minor, fix → patch, anything else none
  • Suggests a corrected message automatically
  • Adjustable maximum subject length (72 by default)

💡 Tips

  • Write the subject in the imperative mood: 'add login', not 'added login'.
  • For breaking changes add ! after the type and a BREAKING CHANGE: footer.
  • If your team uses commitlint, draft messages here first.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a scope required?

No. It helps in monorepos and large projects by showing where the change is, but small projects can skip it.

Can I write in another language?

Yes, as long as the form (type: description) is kept. Agree on style rules like the imperative mood with your team.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. Everything is processed inside your browser; what you enter is never sent to or stored on a server, so you can safely paste data containing personal information, internal settings or API keys.
