💡 Tips

  • Length beats complexity — a long passphrase is more secure than a short mix of symbols.
  • Birthdays, names, "1234", and "qwerty" drastically shorten crack time.
  • Use a different password per service, and a password manager if that's hard to keep track of.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the password I type sent to a server?

No. All analysis happens entirely in your browser and the password you enter is never transmitted anywhere.

How is crack time calculated?

zxcvbn, an algorithm built by Dropbox, analyzes common patterns, dictionary words, and keyboard layouts to estimate the time needed for an offline brute-force attack.

How do I create a strong password?

Length matters more than complexity — aim for 12+ characters of unpredictable word combinations. Avoid birthdays, names, and sequential numbers.
