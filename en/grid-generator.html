💡 Tips

  • 1fr distributes remaining space equally among columns
  • Use repeat(3, 1fr) as shorthand for 3 equal columns
  • minmax(200px, 1fr) sets minimum and maximum sizes
  • auto-fill / auto-fit with minmax() creates responsive grids

FAQ

What is the difference between auto-fill and auto-fit?
Both automatically create as many columns as possible. The difference is that auto-fit collapses empty tracks so items stretch to fill the container, while auto-fill keeps empty tracks.
How do I make a responsive grid without media queries?
Use grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fill, minmax(200px, 1fr)). Items will wrap automatically when the viewport is too narrow.
