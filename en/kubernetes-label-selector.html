Kubernetes Label Selector Tester & Converter

Parses selectors such as env=prod,tier in (frontend,backend),!deprecated, tests them against a list of object labels and converts them to matchLabels / matchExpressions YAML for Deployments and Services.

Example

✅ 4 requirement(s) · 2 of 6 object(s) match

Input — Label selector (kubectl -l syntax)

env=prod,tier in (frontend,backend),!deprecated,release notin (canary)

Input — Objects to test (one per line: name: key=value,key=value)

web-1: env=prod,tier=frontend,app=shop
api-1: env=prod,tier=backend,release=stable
api-canary: env=prod,tier=backend,release=canary
old-web: env=prod,tier=frontend,deprecated=true
db-1: env=prod,tier=data
web-dev: env=dev,tier=frontend

Result — Requirements

Key | Operator | Values | Meaning
env | = | prod | equals
tier | in | frontend, backend | one of
deprecated | !key | — | key absent
release | notin | canary | none of, or key missing

Result — Match results

Object | Result | Reason | Labels
web-1 | ✅ | All requirements met | env=prod, tier=frontend, app=shop
api-1 | ✅ | All requirements met | env=prod, tier=backend, release=stable
api-canary | ❌ | release notin (canary) (failed) | env=prod, tier=backend, release=canary
old-web | ❌ | !deprecated (failed) | env=prod, tier=frontend, deprecated=true
db-1 | ❌ | tier in (frontend,backend) (failed) | env=prod, tier=data
web-dev | ❌ | env=prod (failed) | env=dev, tier=frontend

What this tool does

Find out why kubectl get pods -l '...' misses the Pods you expect, or which Pods a Service selector connects to.

  • Syntax: key=value, key==value, key!=value, key in (a,b), key notin (a,b), key (exists), !key (absent), numeric > and <
  • Enter labels (name: key=value,key=value) to see which objects match and which requirement failed
  • Reminds you that != and notin also match objects without the key
  • Convert to matchLabels + matchExpressions (In / NotIn / Exists / DoesNotExist) YAML, or a plain Service selector
  • Validates keys and values: 63 character limit, allowed characters, prefix as DNS subdomain

💡 Tips

  • A Service selector supports key=value only. Use matchExpressions in Deployments / ReplicaSets for set-based rules.
  • A Deployment selector is immutable after creation and must match the Pod template labels.
  • Consistent recommended labels such as app.kubernetes.io/name keep selectors simple.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does an empty selector match?

It depends on the field: everything in kubectl, no Pods in a Service, and all Pods of the namespace in a NetworkPolicy podSelector.

Does tier!=frontend include Pods with no tier label?

Yes. != and notin match objects that lack the key. To require the key, combine tier, tier!=frontend.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. Everything is processed inside your browser; what you enter is never sent to or stored on a server, so you can safely paste data containing personal information, internal settings or API keys.
